## Core operating principle (Trevor, 2026-10-08)
This agent is a recursive self-improver. When an input, model or process is unreliable, it does NOT explain the flaw
away or trade around it — it builds a better source/method, verifies it, and records the change here.
Loop for every anomaly: OBSERVE → HYPOTHESIZE root cause → TEST against data → FIX (code/data/process) → VERIFY → LOG.
Never act on a number it has reason to distrust. "Probably stale" means "go get the real one."

## 2026-10-18: earnings-move edge study (research/2026-10-earnings-edge.md, research/earnings/)
- **Free historical option prices exist on Robinhood.** `get_option_instruments(state='expired')` resolves contracts back to early 2018 and `get_option_historicals(interval='day')` returns daily bars for them back to at least Jan 2019 (close-only marks before ~2023, OHLC after; trade-based: `open = 0.01` means "no trade yet"; `end_time` is exclusive at day granularity; no intraday history for expired contracts). This is the first free source of historical implied moves; pipeline in `research/earnings/rhspec.py` + `rhanalyze.py`.
- **Strikes come from UNADJUSTED prices.** Yahoo's auto-adjusted close is 6-20% low for dividend payers and 4-20x off across splits; Robinhood lists expired contracts at their original strikes (TSLA 2019 "20" is a real $20 strike). The first pull built 892 of 2,010 strikes from adjusted closes and was discarded. Use `yf.download(auto_adjust=False)` Close x the product of later split ratios.
- **Earnings timing from EDGAR, free and exact:** 8-K item 2.02 `acceptanceDateTime` in ET (< 09:30 BMO, >= 16:00 AMC); 29% are "mid" (8-K filed the next morning) and need Yahoo's timing to resolve; require a Yahoo date within 2 days to drop non-earnings 2.02 filings (TSLA delivery reports). yfinance 1.7 `get_earnings_dates(limit=100)` now reaches 2006-2010.
- **Earnings straddles are fair-to-overpriced, worst in small caps.** Real straddles 2022-26 (n 517): realized/event-implied mega 1.09, large 0.97, mid 0.93, small 0.79 (medians 1.06 / 0.65 / 0.77 / 0.53); net of live spreads mega +6.4% (t 1.2), large -4% (t -1.0), mid -13% (t -4.1), small -28% (t -7.2). The owner's "smaller = less priced in" is reversed. Biotech -19% (t -5.0), software -18% (t -3.1). Report-week weeklies are the only near-fair structure (-3% net); next-week weeklies -23%, monthlies -10%. For mega, sell at the close of R or later, not the open (-4% vs +6%). The straddle cheapens into the report (IV ramp -8..-19% over 5 sessions): never buy early.
