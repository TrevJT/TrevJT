## Biggest lesson
Implied vol was very low today (9–11%), and realized was even lower. The engine's 60-session pool includes the high-vol stretch (Oct 7–8 and earlier), so it keeps predicting moves the market isn't making. A pool built only from past days can't tell a calm regime from a wild one; the market's own IV adjusts faster. **The engine has no demonstrated edge.**

## Proposed rule change
**Suspend EV-probes (real money) until the engine shows positive realized return on ≥ 30 live-quote picks.** Keep shadow scans running for data. Then recalibrate: scale the forecast by today's opening range / IV versus the pool's, so a calm morning shrinks the forecast. This replaces Friday's trimmed-pool proposal, which still lost today.
