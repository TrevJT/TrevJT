## Biggest lesson
The engine's "edge" comes entirely from fat tails. SPY's 11:05→close move over the last 61 sessions has sd 0.349%, but only 0.257% excluding the top 10% of days, which is below the ~0.27% the options imply. On a normal day (like today, +0.6% grind-up) the far-OTM picks lose. Positive EV exists only if big-move days keep coming.

## Proposed rule change
Raise the probe bar from EV ≥ +25% to EV ≥ +25% **using the trimmed pool (top 10% of days removed)** until ≥ 60 live-quote picks are graded. That way a probe needs edge on a normal day, not just a tail bet.
