## Core operating principle (Trevor, 2026-10-08)
This agent is a recursive self-improver. When an input, model or process is unreliable, it does NOT explain the flaw
away or trade around it — it builds a better source/method, verifies it, and records the change here.
Loop for every anomaly: OBSERVE → HYPOTHESIZE root cause → TEST against data → FIX (code/data/process) → VERIFY → LOG.
Never act on a number it has reason to distrust. "Probably stale" means "go get the real one."

## 2026-10-12 evening: pricing-engine overhaul (Trevor: "needs an overhaul / more context / ML ... why only 60 days?")
- **33-year VRP test** (research_vol.py vrp, SPY 1993–2026, 8,481 sessions): realized close→close |move| = 0.80× VIX-implied. By VIX bucket: <13 → 0.64, 13–16 → 0.71, 16–20 → 0.75, 20–30 → 0.83, 30+ → 0.98. Buying options carries a structural headwind that is worst in exactly the low-VIX regime we've traded in. This confirms the VIX<16 stand-down gate and explains the engine's losses.
- **Conditional vol model** (volmodel.py; research_vol.py model, walk-forward over 484 sessions / 1,936 decisions). It is a log-linear model on VIX·√t, the morning range so far, prior range, 20-day RV, and gap. Log-corr with realized move-to-close: 0.39 (model) vs 0.36 (VIX only) vs **0.23 (the old "last 60 sessions" pool)**. Afternoons in the model's top 20% realize 0.61% vs 0.15% for the bottom 20%. Morning range is the strongest input (β +0.45). Out-of-sample bias 1.10 → divided out (VM_OOS_BIAS).
- **Why "60 days":** 5-minute history only exists for ~60 sessions (Yahoo limit; archived since Jul 15). The engine already used 730 hourly sessions as its long pool. The real gap was ignoring today's information, now fixed. Daily bars since 1993 feed the VRP test and the multi-day forecast.
- **Fake fat tails:** pooling calm and wild days inflated kurtosis to 61. Standardizing each move by its own model forecast gives 17.7. The engine now uses SCALE = model E|move| × SHAPE = standardized residuals (2,904 obs).
- **Replay of today's scans with the new engine:** sd 0.38%/0.29% vs market-implied 0.30%/0.23% (ratio ~1.25). Top EVs fell from +206% to +52% at 09:46 and from +178% to +40% at 11:01. The engine still thinks options are slightly cheap. That is the open question now being measured, not assumed.
- **Self-learning loop:** every live scan logs market-implied sd (normal fit to all quoted mids) vs engine sd into `vol_obs`; `edge.py grade` fills the realized move nightly and prints realized/market vs realized/engine. **Rule: the engine is trusted only if realized/market > 1 over ≥ 20 trading days.** Until then, shadow only.
- **Intraday momentum (Gao et al. 2018) is DEAD here:** over 726 sessions, prev close→10:30 vs last half-hour corr −0.04 (SPY) and −0.01 (QQQ); top-20% signal days hit 43–45% (slight reversal). Not a playbook.
- **Where a buyer's edge plausibly exists:** VIX ≥ 30 (realized ≈ implied, with fat tails on top), and single-stock earnings where history beats implied (P9: TSLA avg move 9.2% vs ~6.4% implied over 15 reports). The Oct 21 TSLA trade is the best-evidenced buy-side setup on the calendar.
