# Research sprint 2026-10-07 — three agents (microstructure, events/alt-data, payoff/bankroll)
Bottom line: no tested signal predicts intraday direction. What does work is (1) when to bet (volatility regime, gap days, specific earnings) and (2) how to bet (ATM debit spreads, all-in bold play, stop on target, sell into spikes).
- Volatility: prior VIX ≥ 20 days have 6× the >1.5% moves of VIX 13-16 days. Trade only then.
- Structure: ATM/+$3 SPY 0DTE debit spread −6% EV at VIX≥20 vs −46…−74% for 0.05-0.10Δ singles. Far-OTM lottos are the worst bet despite the big payoffs.
- Sizing: with no edge, bold play (all-in, few bets) maximizes P(target). $143→$1k ≈ 7%, →$5k ≈ 1.2% at best; ruin ≈ 95%.
- Events: TSLA and META earnings have historically moved more than options price (9.2-9.4% vs 6.4-7%). Macro prints are fully priced.
- Gap days (|gap| ≥ 1%): 2.5× the base rate of ≥ 1% moves, direction undetermined.
- Prediction markets: Iran odds shocks precede bigger oil moves (n=19, weak). Watch only.
